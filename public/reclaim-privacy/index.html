Privacy Notice

Reclaim Disk Space for macOS

Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version

Reclaim collects nothing. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no server. Nothing about your files — not their names, not their sizes, not their paths, not a count of them — ever leaves your Mac.

What Reclaim reads

Reclaim reads the names, sizes and modification dates of files in folders you explicitly give it access to, in order to show you what is using your space. macOS will not let it read anything you have not handed over: you choose a disk or folder through the standard Open panel, or by dragging it onto the window.

Reclaim never reads the contents of your files. It only ever asks the filesystem how much room each one takes up.

What Reclaim stores

  • Access grants. When you give Reclaim a folder, macOS issues a bookmark so you do not have to grant it again. These are stored in the app's own container on your Mac.
  • Scan records. So Reclaim can tell you what grew since last time, it keeps a small local record of folder paths and sizes from your previous scan. This stays in the app's container and is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Preferences. Your chosen view and colour settings.

All of this is on your own machine. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

Network access

Reclaim connects to the network for exactly one purpose, and which one depends on where you got it.

  • From the Mac App Store: to talk to Apple's App Store, to process and restore the one-time Reclaim Pro purchase. That transaction is handled entirely by Apple. Reclaim never sees your payment details, and Apple's own privacy policy governs it.
  • Downloaded directly: to check for a new version. Your licence key is checked on your Mac, by verifying a signature — it is never sent anywhere, and Reclaim works offline forever once it is entered.

There is no analytics and no crash reporter in either build.

Deleting files

Reclaim only ever moves items to the Trash, and only ones you have explicitly selected and confirmed. It never deletes anything permanently, never removes anything automatically, and refuses outright to touch system files.

Children

Reclaim is a utility with no accounts, no content and no communication features, and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
