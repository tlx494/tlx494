Steady: Metronome & Tuner, for iPhone, iPad and Android

I bought Pro and it isn't showing

Open Settings (the sliders icon at the top right), tap Unlock Steady Pro, then Restore purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to the Apple Account or Google account that bought it, so restore while signed in to that same account. It carries over to any phone or tablet on that account at no extra cost.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a single payment that unlocks Pro for good. The metronome and tuner themselves are free, with no ads.

The click stops when the screen locks

Steady is built to keep playing in the background, with play and stop on the lock screen. If it stops anyway on Android, the phone is probably closing it to save battery: open Settings › Apps › Steady › Battery and choose Unrestricted (the exact names vary a little between phone makers).

The tuner doesn't react

It needs the microphone. On iPhone, open Settings › Steady and turn on Microphone. On Android, open Settings › Apps › Steady › Permissions and allow it. Play one note at a time, close to the phone.

What's the difference between the app and the web tools?

The web tools are free to use in any browser, but on a phone they stop when the screen locks or the browser closes. The app keeps playing with the screen off, keeps your practice history, saves presets and setlists, and works offline.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds do too. See the refund policy for the steps on each store.

Anything else

Found a bug, or want a feature? Use Send feedback in the app's Settings, or email keyboardappdev@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.
