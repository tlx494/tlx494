Support

Need help? Email keyboardappdev@gmail.com. Include your device, operating system, app version and what happened. Please do not send passwords or card details.

How do I play?

Hold to grow a shape and release to drop it. It must be wide enough to rest on the ledges but no wider than the hole. Press once more while it falls to top it up. Perfects and Greats build combos; Perfects also earn lives. Holding too far uses a limited size reset.

Where are my purchases?

Use Restore purchases in Settings or at the bottom of the Shop. Check you are signed into the same Apple or Google account used to buy them. Store purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android. Scores, stars and earned items are local and may be lost when you clear app storage.

Why do I still see an ad after Remove Ads?

Remove Ads stops automatic interstitials. Ads you choose to watch for a reward remain available. The preparation build uses Google's clearly marked test ads.

How do I change privacy choices?

Settings → Privacy choices appears where Google's consent SDK requires it. You can also change tracking or advertising ID settings on your device. See the privacy policy for details, or email us with a privacy or deletion request.

Daily runs and accessibility

The daily run changes at midnight UTC. Each player receives one free attempt, with an optional rewarded-ad second attempt. Sound, haptics and reduced motion can be changed in Settings. Menus and on-screen text are available in English, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
