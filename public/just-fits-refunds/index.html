Refunds

Last updated: 13 November 2026. All Just Fits: Shape Drop purchases are processed by Apple or Google Play. Keep your store receipt.

Apple App Store

Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, using the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple decides and processes App Store refund requests; the developer cannot issue an Apple refund on your behalf.

Google Play

Use Google Play's refund request process. Eligibility depends on the purchase and the time since payment. For a purchase problem or a request outside Google's self-service period, email keyboardappdev@gmail.com with your Google Play order number. Do not send payment-card details.

Restore before buying again

If an item is missing, first use Restore purchases in Settings or the Shop while signed into the same store account. Do not buy it again to fix a restore problem.

Consumer guarantees

Nothing here limits rights or remedies under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer laws that cannot be excluded. A stated store process or time window does not remove those rights.
