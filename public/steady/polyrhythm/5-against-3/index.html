How to count 5 against 3

Count 15 even slots per cycle: the 5 lands on 1, 4, 7, 10 and 13, the 3 on 1, 6 and 11. Together they make the rhythm 1, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11, 13.

The two voices meet only on the downbeat. Learn the five on its own first, evenly across three slow beats, before adding the click for the three.

Press Start to hear it, mute either voice to practise one part against the other, and change the numbers above to try any other ratio.
