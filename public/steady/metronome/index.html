The Steady app does what a browser can’t: it keeps playing with the screen off, keeps your practice history and streaks, saves setlists, has a gap trainer and works offline.

App Store Google Play

How to use it

Drag the ruler on the right, or tap the number and type a tempo, then press Start or the space bar. Tap any beat to cycle it between accented, normal and silent. Time sets the meter and Subdiv adds eighths, triplets or sixteenths you can hear.

Clicks are scheduled on the browser’s audio clock a fraction of a second ahead, not fired by a timer, so a busy page or a slow phone does not push them late. Compound meters such as 6/8 and 12/8 accent each group of three by default.
