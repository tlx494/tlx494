How to tune

Press Start tuner and allow the microphone, then play one note at a time. The needle shows how many cents (hundredths of a semitone) you are sharp or flat; within five cents either way the note is in tune.

The reference defaults to A = 440 Hz. Set 415 Hz for Baroque pitch, or 442 to 443 Hz to match many European orchestras. The sound is analysed inside your browser and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
