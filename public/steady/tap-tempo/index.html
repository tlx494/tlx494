Use in metronome

The Steady app does what a browser can’t: it keeps playing with the screen off, keeps your practice history and streaks, saves setlists, has a gap trainer and works offline.

App Store Google Play

How it counts

Tap along with the beat, on the pad or on any key. The reading is the average of your last eight taps, so it settles after a few beats. Pause for two seconds and it starts a fresh count; a tap far off the beat restarts from the tap before it rather than dragging the average.

When the number holds steady, Use in metronome carries it across.
