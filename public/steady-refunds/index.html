Refund Policy — Steady

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Steady is a metronome and tuner app made by Daniel O'Dea. This policy covers Steady Pro, the app's one-time optional upgrade.

Short version

If Steady Pro is not what you expected, ask for your money back within 30 days and I will help you get it. If it has been longer than that, ask anyway and tell me what went wrong — I would rather refund someone than have them stuck with an app they do not want.

Try it before you pay

Steady's core is free and stays free: the metronome, the tuner, subdivisions, polyrhythms and your practice history cost nothing, and there are no ads. Pro only adds to that, so you can decide whether the app suits you before any money changes hands.

Bought on Android (Google Play)

Google Play processes the payment, so the refund goes through Google:

  • Within 48 hours you can refund the purchase yourself, without asking anyone: open your Google Play order history, find Steady Pro and request the refund there.
  • After 48 hours the self-service option disappears, but I can still refund the order from the Play Console. Email keyboardappdev@gmail.com from the address you bought it with, or use the feedback form in the app, and I will take care of it.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, on Google's timing — usually a few business days, depending on your bank.

Bought on iPhone or iPad (App Store)

Apple handles every App Store payment, and Apple does not let a developer refund those purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, where Apple decides under its own refund policy.

If Apple turns down a request you think is fair, write to me anyway — I can support the request, and it is worth telling me what went wrong regardless.

What a refund changes

A refunded purchase is no longer licensed for use.

Nothing else is touched. Your presets, sequences, custom sounds, recordings and practice history live on your device, not on a server, and a refund does not delete any of it. If you buy Pro again later, it unlocks again — you are never asked to pay twice on the same store account.

Your consumer rights

This policy sits on top of your legal rights, it does not replace them. Nothing here excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law, or under the consumer protection laws of your own country, that cannot lawfully be excluded. If Steady has a major problem, you are entitled to a refund whatever the 30 days says.

Contact

Use the feedback form inside the app, or email keyboardappdev@gmail.com. A real person reads it.
