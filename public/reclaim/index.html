Reclaim Disk Space

Find what is actually eating your Mac's storage, and get it back.

Point it at a disk. It measures every folder the way du does — what files really occupy, not what Finder rounds them to — and draws the result two ways at once: a sunburst you read by shape, and a treemap you read by area. Alongside it is a list of things that are genuinely safe to remove, each one explaining what it costs you.

Nothing is ever erased. Everything goes to the Trash, so anything can be put back.

Which one?

They are the same app, built from the same source. The difference is the sandbox.

  • A sandboxed app cannot be given Full Disk Access. The sandbox is checked before that setting, so turning it on in System Settings does nothing — which is why the App Store version reports those folders as skipped rather than pretending it measured them.
  • The direct download has no sandbox, so one grant makes all of it measurable, and external drives readable with no per-drive prompt.
  • Everything else — the maps, the reclaim rules, the Trash-only removal — is identical.

Your licence

A direct-download licence is a signed key, checked on your own Mac. It is never sent anywhere, it works offline, and it keeps working on every Mac you own. There is no account, no activation server, and nothing to lose access to if this site ever goes away.
