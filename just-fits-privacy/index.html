Privacy policy

Just Fits: Shape Drop is made by Daniel O'Dea. Contact keyboardappdev@gmail.com. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Your game stays on your device

Scores, stars, daily attempts, purchases restored from the store and settings are stored locally. Just Fits: Shape Drop has no account system or developer-operated player database. It does not request your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress; non-consumable purchases can be restored from the same store account.

To pace ads, the game keeps a small record on your device of how ads went (when one was shown, whether you kept playing afterwards, whether an ad could load) and uses it only to decide how often you see ads. That record is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and is erased when you clear the game's data or uninstall. Where Google's consent form applies and you have not allowed information to be stored on your device, it is kept only while the game is open.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The mobile app includes Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs. These services may collect and share device identifiers (including an advertising ID when permitted), approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising data, app interactions, crash reports and performance diagnostics. Google and its advertising partners use this data to serve and measure ads, operate and improve their services, and detect fraud. Test builds use Google's test ad IDs.

The game also uses Google's Firebase Analytics to count how it is played and how ads go: for example how long runs last, whether an offered ad was watched, and whether a player kept playing after an ad. These records carry a random installation number and no name, account or email. We use them to tune the game and how often ads appear, and individual records are deleted after two months. Where Google's consent form applies, they are sent only if you agreed; everywhere, Settings has a switch, "Share usage statistics", that turns them off and resets the installation number.

The game's AdMob and Firebase Analytics accounts are linked. Google therefore adds ad events to the same usage statistics (that an ad was shown or tapped, and the estimated revenue from it), and can use those statistics inside AdMob to report on how ads perform and to improve them. The link adds no name, account or email, and the "Share usage statistics" switch turns these records off as well.

These SDKs may transfer data outside Australia. Google's retention and deletion practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and information about partner apps. Google encrypts SDK data in transit. We do not promise that denying personalised ads stops every SDK data transfer.

Your choices

The game requests consent through Google's consent flow where required, before requesting ads. On iOS, tracking is also subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Refusing tracking does not stop you playing. Where Google requires it, Settings → Privacy choices lets you reopen the privacy form. You can change iOS tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, use Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Google also provides My Ad Center.

Remove Ads stops automatic interstitials. Rewarded ads remain optional and still use the ads SDK if you choose to watch them. The game does not show banner or app-open ads.

Purchases and sharing

Apple or Google processes payments under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your card details. The game reads store purchase status to grant and restore items. Sharing is optional and opens your device's share sheet; information you send is handled by the service you choose.

Support, deletion and young players

If you email support, we receive the information you send and use it to respond. Contact us to request access to or deletion of support correspondence. We cannot identify or delete independently retained Google advertising records; use Google's privacy controls for that data. Just Fits: Shape Drop is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Contact us about privacy concerns or data rights at keyboardappdev@gmail.com.
